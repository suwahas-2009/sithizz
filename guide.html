📖 How To Buy? - Full Guide

How to buy an account? Done in just 10 minutes!

1

🎮 Choose An Account

Browse the Account List on the Home Page and pick one by checking the Level, Price and Skins. You can only buy accounts marked as Available. Sold accounts are already taken.

2

💬 Click Buy Now

Click the Buy Now button under the account. It will open my WhatsApp directly with a message. You don't need to type anything, the account name will be sent automatically.

3

💰 Make The Payment

I will send you the Payment Details. You can pay via BOC Bank Transfer. All Bank Details are on the Payment Page.

4

🧾 Send Screenshot

After you pay, send the Payment Screenshot to WhatsApp. As soon as I see the screenshot, I will confirm your payment.

5

🔑 Get Login Details

Within 10 Minutes you will receive the Full Login Details of the account. Gmail / Facebook Login + Password, everything. After that you can change the password too.

6

✅ Login To The Game

Open Free Fire and Login. After you login, make sure to change your Password and Recovery Details. Then the account will be 100% yours.

⚡ Pro Tip: If you message after 10 PM, you will get a reply in the morning. If urgent, type "URGENT" on WhatsApp.
